**Problem 1:**

Consider the polynomial $$p(x) = 2x + 2$$.  In which of the following rings it irreducible?

* (a) $$\mathbb{Z}[x]$$
* (b) $$\mathbb{Q}[x]$$

**Problem 2:**

Consider the ring $$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-3}]$$.

* (a) Show that $$1+\sqrt{-3}$$ is irreducible.
* (b) Show that $$2$$ is irreducible
* (c) Show that $$1+\sqrt{-3}$$ is not prime.
* (d) Is $$2$$ prime?  Explain.
* (e) Explain why $$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-3}]$$ is not a UFD.

**Problem 3:**

Consider the ring of Gaussian integers $$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-1}]$$.

* (a) Find all the units.
* (b) Suppose that $$z,w\in \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-1}]$$ are nonzero and satisfy $$\lvert z\rvert > \lvert w\rvert$$ where here $$\lvert\cdot\rvert$$ is the complex absolute value.
Prove that there exist $$x,y\in \mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-1}]$$ with

$$z = yw + x,\quad\text{and}\quad \lvert x\rvert < \lvert w\rvert.$$

This shows that $$\mathbb{Z}[\sqrt{-1}]$$ has some version of the **division algorithm**, making it what is called a **Euclidean domain**.

* (c) Use (b) to determine a greatest common divisor of $$442+182i$$ and $$-221+91i$$, where $$i=\sqrt{-1}$$.

**Problem 4:**

The ring of **Laurent polynomials** with rational coefficients is the set $$\mathbb{Q}[x,x^{-1}]$$ of all "polynomials with positive and negative powers" ie. elements of the form

$$a_{-m}x^{-m} + a_{1-m}x^{1-m} + \dots + a_{-1}x^{-1} + a_0x + a_1x^1 + \dots + a_{n-1}x^{n-1} + a_nx^n$$

for any integers $$m,n$$ and elements $$a_{-m},\dots, a_m\in \mathbb{Q}$$.

* (a)  Prove that if $$a,b\in\mathbb{Q}$$ are both nonzero, then $$a+bx$$ is not a unit in $$\mathbb{Q}[x,x^{-1}]$$
* (b)  Is $$x + x^{-1}$$ irreducible in $$\mathbb{Q}[x,x^{-1}]$$?  Carefully explain.
