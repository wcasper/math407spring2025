Solve each of the following problems.
If the problem asks for a proof, be sure to carefully justify your work, including any theorems from class.

Note: you may NOT use a theorem or result from class to prove something when it makes the problem entirely trivial.  If you are unsure whether a particular theorem or result is allowed, just ask!

## Problem 1 (True or False)
For each of the following, write TRUE if the statement is true and FALSE if the statement is false.  NO explanation is needed.

a) if $$G$$ is a subgroup of order $$26$$, then $$G$$ is isomorphic to $$\mathbb{Z}_{26}$$ or $$D_{13}$$

b) in an integral domain, every prime is irreducible

c) the smallest non-Abelian group is $$S_2$$

d) every Abelian group is a direct product of cyclic groups

e) if $$n$$ divides the order of a group $$G$$, then $$G$$ has an element of order $$n$$

## Problem 2

a) Write down, up to isomorphism, all Abelian groups of order $$72$$.

b) Write $$\mathbb Z_3\times \mathbb{Z}_4\times\mathbb{Z}_6$$ in prime divisor form

c) Write $$\mathbb Z_3\times \mathbb{Z}_4\times\mathbb{Z}_6$$ in invariant factor form

## Problem 3

Let $$G=\mathbb{Z}\times\mathbb{Z}$$ and let $$H$$ be the subgroup of $$G$$ generated by $$(1,1)$$ and $$(2,0)$$.

a) Write down explicitly the distinct cosets of $$G/H$$

b) Determine what finite Abelian group $$G/H$$ is isomorphic to, in invariant factor form

## Problem 4

a) State the definition of two elements in a ring being associates

b) Define the greatest common divisor of two elements $$a,b$$ in an integral domain $$R$$.

c) Give an example of an integral domain with two elements which do not have a greatest common divisor

## Problem 5

Let $$G$$ be a cyclic group and suppose that $$N$$ is a normal subgroup of $$G$$.
Prove that $$G/N$$ is also a cyclic group.

## Problem 6

Prove that if $$G$$ is a finite Abelian group and $$n$$ divides the order of $$G$$, then $$G$$ has an element of order $$n$$.
Hint: use the Structure Theorem in prime divisor form.

## Problem 7

a) State the First Isomorphism Theorem for Groups
b) Explain why

$$\text{SL}_2(\mathbb{R}) = \left\lbrace\left(\begin{array}{cc}a & b\\c & d\end{array}\right): ad-bc=1\right\rbrace$$

is a normal subgroup of 

$$\text{GL}_2(\mathbb{R}) = \left\lbrace\left(\begin{array}{cc}a & b\\c & d\end{array}\right): ad-bc\neq 0\right\rbrace$$

c) Prove that

$$\text{GL}_2(\mathbb{R})/\text{SL}_2(\mathbb{R})\cong\mathbb{R}.$$
