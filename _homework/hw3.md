**Problem 1:**

Let $$G = \mathbb{Z}_4\times\mathbb{Z}_4$$ and $$N$$ be the cyclic subgroup generated by $$(3,2)$$.

a) Explicitly write out the subsets of $$G$$ which form the elements of $$G/N$$.
b) Write out an addition table for $$G/N$$.
c) Prove that $$G/N$$ is isomorphic as a group to $$\mathbb{Z}_4$$.

**Problem 2:**

Consider the subset $$N$$ of $$S_4$$ defined by

$$N = \{e,(12)(34),(13)(24),(14)(23)\}.$$

a) Prove that $$N$$ is a normal subgroup of $$S_4$$
b) Explicitly describe the cosets of $$S_4/N$$
c) Write out a multiplication table for the quotient group $$S_4/N$$.


**Problem 3:** 

Suppose that $$\varphi: G\rightarrow H$$ are groups and that $$N$$ is a normal subgroup of $$G$$.

Consider the function

$$\overline{\varphi}: G/N\rightarrow H,\quad g\ast N\mapsto \varphi(g).$$

This function may or may not be well-defined.  When it is, we say $$\varphi$$ **descends to the quotient**.

a) Suppose $$G=\mathbb{R}^3$$, $$H = \mathbb{R}^2$$, 

$$N=\text{span}\left\lbrace\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ 1 \\ 1\end{array}\right]\right\rbrace,$$

and let $$\varphi: G\rightarrow H$$ be the linear transformation defined by

$$\varphi:
\left[\begin{array}{c}x \\ y \\ z\end{array}\right]\mapsto
\left[\begin{array}{c}
x + y - 2z\\
2x + 2y -4z
\end{array}\right]$$

Show that $$\overline{\varphi}$$ well-defined.

b) Suppose $$G$$, $$H$$, and $$\varphi$$ are the same as before, but 

$$N=\text{span}\left\lbrace\left[\begin{array}{c}1 \\ 0 & 1\\\end{array}\right]\right\rbrace,$$

Prove that $$\overline{\varphi}$$ is no longer well-defined.

c) Prove in general that if $$N\subseteq\ker(\varphi)$$, then $$\overline{\varphi}$$ is well-defined.

**Problem 4:**

Let $$\mathcal M$$ be the Mobius group from Homework 1.
Consider the function

$$\psi: \text{GL}_2(\mathbb C)\rightarrow \mathcal M,\quad
\psi\left(\left[\begin{array}{cc}
a & b\\ c & d
\end{array}\right]\right) = \frac{az + b}{cz + d}.$$

a) Prove that $$\psi$$ is a group epimorphism
b) Prove that $$\mathcal M$$ is isomorphic to the projective general linear group
c) Determine the value of $$\chi^{2025}(1)$$, where $$\chi(z) = \frac{3z-1}{-z+3}$$.  [Hint: matrix diagonalization!]

$$\text{PSL}_2(\mathbb C) = \text{GL}_2(\mathbb C)/N,$$

where here $$N$$ is the normal subgroup of $$\text{GL}_2(\mathbb C)$$ consisting of all scalar multiples of the identity matrix.

**Problem 5:**

In this problem, we will prove **Cayley's Theorem** that every finite group turns out to be isomorphic to a subgroup of a symmetric group.

Let $$G$$ be a group with $$n$$ elements

$$G = \{g_1,g_2,\dots, g_n\}.$$

For each fixed integer $$1\leq j\leq n$$, define a function

$$\sigma_j: \{1,2,\dots,n\}\rightarrow\{1,2,\dots,n\}$$

by letting $$\sigma_j(k)$$ be the unique integer with $$g_jg_k = g_{\sigma_j(k)}$$.

a) Prove that $$\sigma_j$$ is actually a bijection, and hence an element of $$S_n$$.
b) Prove that 

$$\sigma_i(\sigma_j(k)) = \sigma_{\sigma_i(j)}(k).$$

c) Prove that the function $$\varphi: G\rightarrow S_n$$ defined by

$$\varphi(g_j) = \sigma_j,\quad\text{for all}\ 1\leq j\leq n$$

is a monomorphism.
d) Use the **First Isomorphism Theorem** to explain why (c) implies $$G$$ is isomorphic to a subgroup of $$S_n$$
